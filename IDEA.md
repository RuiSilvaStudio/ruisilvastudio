This project is to re-build Rui Silva Studio website that currently live in squarespace at ruisilvastudio.com. Rui Silva Studio is a contemporary design practice dedicated to creating one-of-a-kind collectible furniture. Founded in 2024 by designer Rui Silva after more than 15 years in senior leadership within the luxury fashion industry, the studio brings a unique perspective to the world of furniture: one that blends craftsmanship, cultural storytelling, and luxury-level detail.

At the heart of the studio’s philosophy is the belief that furniture can be art. Each creation is handcrafted in limited numbers or as a unique piece, using premium materials such as white oak, brass, steel, porcelain, and others. The process is guided by precision, patience, and the pursuit of timeless design.

Drawing from Rui Silva’s experience as SVP of Creative Operations at FARFETCH, where he collaborated with the world’s leading fashion houses, the studio applies the same discipline, creativity, and commitment to excellence to every piece it produces.

The result is furniture that is not mass-produced, but crafted to endure - objects designed to be lived with, admired, and collected. Rui Silva Studio stands at the intersection of design, culture, and craftsmanship, offering work that resonates with curators, interior designers, collectors, and design-conscious audiences.
